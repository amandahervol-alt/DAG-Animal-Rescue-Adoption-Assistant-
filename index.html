🚐 Out-of-State Adoptions

Yes, DAG adopts out-of-state!

We work with compassionate adopters across the US. We conduct virtual meet-and-greets and help coordinate ground or flight transport arrangements for approved homes.

  • Virtual Home & Yard Check
  • Veterinary Reference Check
  • Direct Transport Coordination

📝 4-Step Adoption Roadmap

  1. Submit Application online or at an adoption event.
  2. Volunteer Review within 24–48 hours.
  3. Meet & Greet with the animal and your family.
  4. Adoption Contract & Welcome Home!

🏡 Fostering Saves Lives

Fostering gives shelter animals a quiet, loving space to decompress while waiting for their forever homes. DAG is a foster-based rescue and relies on community homes.

🩺 100% Medical Coverage: DAG covers all veterinary care, medications, and spay/neuter surgery for foster animals!
